EMBRACE / poetry × technological change
01 / Before reading

What changed your world?

Think about the past five years. Which part of your life changed most because of technology?

Give one concrete example.

Classroom move: quick show of hands after each choice. There is no right answer yet.

02 / Prediction

The title is Embrace.

Before reading, what do you think the speaker wants us to do with technological change?

03 / First reading

Read for the big idea.

Use the Full poem button. Read once without stopping. Then choose the statement that best captures the speaker’s position.

04 / Image & metaphor

What picture does the poem create?

“spreading its wire like tentacles”

What does this image suggest about technology?

05 / Evidence hunt

True, false — and prove it.

Decide T/F. Then use the poem to justify each answer aloud with a line or phrase.

Technological change is presented as fast and difficult to reverse.
The speaker is unwilling to accept technological change.
Everyday life is largely unaffected by technology.
The reader is urged not to be left behind by change.
06 / Language

08 / Tone

How does the speaker sound?

Choose the strongest answer, then defend it.

09 / Agree or challenge

Should we always “embrace” new technology?

Move from 1 to 5: 1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree.

10 / Two sides

Opportunity or risk?

Tap each idea to classify it. Use the class discussion to challenge any placement.

Risk

11 / Rewrite

Change the speaker’s message.

Write one line that answers the poem from a more cautious point of view. Start with one of these:

Before we embrace it…Change can help us, but…We should not fear technology; however…
12 / Mini debate

30 seconds per side

Side A
The poem is right: resisting technological change leaves us behind.
Side B
The poem is too optimistic: some technologies should be slowed, limited, or refused.

Use: claim → example → because.

13 / Exit ticket

Finish in one sentence.

The poem’s message is powerful because… / limited because…
